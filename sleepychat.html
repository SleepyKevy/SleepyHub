SLEEPYCHAT

The focused chat side of SleepyHub: keep Twitch and Kick conversations together in one clean creator-first experience.

What SleepyChat is

SleepyChat is the focused chat companion in the SleepyHub ecosystem. Its job is much simpler than SleepySource: keep live chat easy to read and manage, with Twitch and Kick conversations brought together in one clean experience.

Designed around chat

  • Combined Twitch + Kick chat view
  • Platform-specific views when you only want one chat
  • Send or reply without constantly switching apps
  • Support for recognizable chat identity such as names, badges, colors, and emotes
  • Clear connection state and automatic reconnection behavior
  • Simple scrolling controls for fast-moving chat
  • A clean dark interface that stays readable while live

How it differs from SleepySource

SleepyChat stays intentionally focused on conversation. SleepySource is the larger streaming toolkit; SleepyChat is the lighter chat-first companion.
